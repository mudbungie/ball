Maintainer's attack on bl-3616 §2 claim 1 (2026-09-06): 'locally I will have a bunch of agents running with subagents, so balls is a multi-writer blast radius even at the local scale. It's never single-writer.' Concede. The invariant that actually makes ff-only sufficient today is not single-writer; it is 'a rejected push un-seals, so the local store is never ahead of the remote by more than the in-flight op.' Deferred publication breaks that invariant on purpose. Amend docs/design/bl-3616-store-tiers.md §2/§3/§6: reconcile = fetch + rebase local seals + push; per-op push on reject rebases the ONE in-flight seal and retries once (E5 narrows to same-ball conflict); opt-in sync rebases N seals with the same code; occupancy (claim/unclaim) may stay eager while content defers — per-hook wiring, still no mode.
