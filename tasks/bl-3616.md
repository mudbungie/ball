Design dialogue (PROPOSED). Deliverable: docs/design/<id>-store-tiers.md. The maintainer's reframe: balls lifecycles at high velocity locally under the user's name; shared stores get DELIBERATELY checked-in work; the corporate tracker (jira/gh issues) is the next tier up with roadmap goals; a ball's counterpart one tier up is sometimes a direct mirror, sometimes the whole of which it is a component. Wanted: bidirectional pointers to balls in other stores/repos and to external trackers (canonical, plugin-resolved), plus a sync-policy ladder — mandatory replication (today), warnings at various obnoxiousness levels, explicit opt-in sync. Thesis: no new field, no mode knob, no new verb — publication = bl sync; 'unsynced' = a derived ahead/behind query; every tier boundary = one plugin instance on the same hooks; the ladder = which hooks it is wired on.
